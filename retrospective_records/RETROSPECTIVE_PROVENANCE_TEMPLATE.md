## 3. Reconstruction standard

This record distinguishes:

1. **Contemporaneously documented fact** — directly supported by a dated record created during the work.
2. **Retrospective reconstruction** — a present-day synthesis based on multiple contemporaneous records.
3. **Current scientific interpretation** — an interpretation developed or refined after the historical work.
4. **Unresolved uncertainty** — a point that cannot be established reliably from available records.

Do not infer undocumented actions or assign another person's contribution without evidence.
