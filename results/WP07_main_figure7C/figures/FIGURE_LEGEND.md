# Main Figure 7C legend

**Coordinated views of reference glycan chemotypes and viral GT-associated communities.** Identical saved joint UMAP coordinates are displayed in two maps with common limits and equal axis units. Left, 195 reference BGCs carrying an original Saccharide label are colored by the seven frozen MIBiG 3.1 primary-chemotype display groups used in Supplementary Figure 2. All 65 Saccharide-containing hybrid references are retained; unresolved and provisional annotations are unchanged. The other 2,307 references are light gray, and 287 viral loci appear as faint hollow triangles for positional context. Right, the same viral loci are colored by their original viral-only Leiden communities VC01-VC05 (68, 55, 74, 35 and 55 loci); Saccharide references are pale-gold landmarks and other references remain light gray. Open colored triangles identify VC04 and VC05 as a display convention. Each map contains the same 2,789 unique samples; repeated positions across panels are not additional observations. The original joint UMAP included 2,502 references and all 556 viral loci. The 269 unselected viral loci are omitted without refitting. Viral-only communities were defined in the original 320-dimensional representation, independently of two-dimensional layout; reference chemotypes came separately from MIBiG records. Both proximity and community organization depend on the learned representation, trained using broad reference biosynthetic labels. Spatial correspondence does not establish identical biochemical functions or predict viral glycan products.
