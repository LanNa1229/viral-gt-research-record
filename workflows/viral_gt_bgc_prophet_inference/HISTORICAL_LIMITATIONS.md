No original pilot protein cache or LMDB was read by the regression. The all-actual-gene pooling and original unused-positional-encoding behavior are retained.

All such denials were examined and involve only the new audit harness, with no unregistered scientific reads or network/subprocess calls. Raw audit and logs remain in the local preparation evidence; the compact release report records this adjudication. The regression was not rerun merely to remove those benign diagnostic denials.
