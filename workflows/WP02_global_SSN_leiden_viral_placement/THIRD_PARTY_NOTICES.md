Distributed output remains subject to the included `licenses/ALPHAFOLD3_OUTPUT_TERMS_OF_USE.md` and conspicuous `licenses/Legally Binding Terms of Use.txt` notice. ## Modifications and publication status

Original structure bytes, filenames and relative paths are preserved. The curators' existing processing/selection notes remain in metadata; their absence does not establish that an upstream model was unmodified. Release preparation adds manifests, metadata joins, source classes, documentation and external licensing notices, without changing coordinates. Scientific code and original numerical outputs are preserved.

The complete local archive includes unresolved files for preservation and validation, not public redistribution. Do not upload/publish this complete archive until applicable terms for those 2,237 files are established and explicit publication approval is given. Source uncertainty is the remaining deposition blocker. AlphaFold 3's specific output terms are an inclusion/notice requirement, not a categorical prohibition on scientific deposition. A single dataset-wide unrestricted CC BY 4.0 label would be misleading; use a mixed-rights/custom-license description at deposition and retain the file-level map.

Official terms were checked and downloaded on 2026-10-28. AlphaFold 3 included terms are marked last modified 2024-11-09. See the included exact terms; do not substitute a short summary for them.
