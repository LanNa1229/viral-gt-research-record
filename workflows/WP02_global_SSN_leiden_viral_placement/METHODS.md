# Global structure-similarity network and viral placement

We assembled 4,854 curated GT-A-like protein structures comprising viral candidates and cross-domain references. Exhaustive comparisons were performed with Foldseek (commit 19800e921de4dcadd646feac40d83b4114137515) using TM-align, a TM-score threshold of 0.65 and bidirectional coverage of 0.50, without an E-value cutoff. After identifier correction and removal of self-alignments, directional records were collapsed into 224,825 undirected pairs. Pair weights were the mean, across available directions, of the smaller query- and target-length-normalized TM-score. Communities were inferred with weighted Leiden RBConfiguration partitioning at resolution 1.0 and seed 1, iterating until convergence. CAZy annotations and taxonomy informed post hoc interpretation. Viral placement classification (v3.0.0) combined community composition with weighted node topology. Direct family support for CAZy-unassigned proteins required at least two same-family annotated neighbors within their community, a family-specific annotated-neighbor strength fraction ≥0.67 and a best-versus-second-family margin ≥0.20. Display-network reduction was confined to visualization; quantitative analyses used the full graph.

Technical parameters, classification rules and the limitation on de novo historical partition regeneration are in [Supplementary Methods](SUPPLEMENTARY_METHODS.md). The preserved primary partition is the manuscript reference dataset.
